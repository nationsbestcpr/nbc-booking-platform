What this will do

    {% if action == 'transfer' %}
  1. Pick the new class from the same city's classes with seats (the same search as the website).
  2. The rules work out whether the move is free, has a fee, or has a price difference to charge or refund.
  3. The seat moves in one step: it can't be lost or double-booked on the way. The old class gets the seat back.
  4. GHL: the same opportunity is updated with the new date and place, so the customer gets the "class changed" message.
  5. Recorded in the booking's history and the action log, with who did it and why.
    6. {% elif action == 'cancel' %}
  6. Choose why: the customer asked, under-24-hour notice, late arrival, duplicate booking.
  7. The rules say whether they get money back, and how much; anything to refund goes to the Refund step.
  8. The seat goes back on sale at once (or to the first person on the waiting list).
  9. GHL: the opportunity moves to Cancelled, which sends the cancellation message.
  10. Recorded in the booking's history and the action log.
    11. {% else %}
  11. The rules suggest the amount for this seat; the desk can lower it, not raise it beyond what was paid.
  12. Above the manager threshold, the refund waits under Refund approvals for a manager; the database refuses it otherwise.
  13. Sent to Stripe (test mode on the test site); the card is refunded and the receipt comes from Stripe.
  14. Refunds done straight in the Stripe dashboard are caught too, so the numbers here stay right.
  15. Recorded in the booking's history and the action log.
    16. {% endif %}
