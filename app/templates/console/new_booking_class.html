2a. Caller pays by card

The seat is held while we text and email them a secure payment link (Stripe). Card details are never typed into this site.

  1. Enter the buyer and each attendee (name and email; phone for texts).
  2. Send the payment link: the seats are held for the same time as an online booking.
  3. Once they pay, it's the same as an online booking: confirmation from GHL, seat on their account.

2b. Company pays by invoice

For employers and groups: seats are confirmed now and the money is due by the invoice date.

  1. Company, billing contact, PO number if they have one.
  2. Each attendee (name and email).
  3. Stripe sends the invoice; the booking shows "Payment due" until it's paid.
